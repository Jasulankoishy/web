Look at the menu

This is a class project about SF Shaurma Food. Here you can look at some dishes, check prices and plan a visit. You can open these pages from a folder on your computer. You cannot place an order here.

Start with the «Menu» page. The table shows shawarma, burgers and ayran, with prices from the SF page on Wolt. Check the portion size: a larger portion can cost more. Make sure you choose the one you want.

Other pages

  1. Look at the dishes and prices.
  2. Open Visit and choose a city.
  3. Find a branch on the official website.
  4. Try the practice feedback form.

About SF tells you about the food. The Gallery shows three photos we took outside SF. FAQ answers questions about pickup, locations and the forms. The Colophon explains how the HTML pages work.

Use the official SF website to place a real order. Check the address and opening hours before you go. The restaurant may change its prices, and some dishes may be sold out.
