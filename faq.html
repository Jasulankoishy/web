Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions

Where can I see the menu?
Visit the official SF website. Our table shows only a few dishes. Check the prices and ask if your dish is available before you order.
Can I pick up my order?
Yes, the SF website has pickup and delivery options. Use that site for a real order.
Where is the nearest SF?
Look at the locations on the SF website. Choose one near you and check its address and opening hours.
Does this form send anything?
No. It has no server. The browser checks the required fields and opens this page again. Your details and question are not sent or saved.
